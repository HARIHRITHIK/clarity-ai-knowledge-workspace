AGENDA ITEMS

1. APPROVAL OF PRIOR MEETING MINUTES

James Patterson moved to approve the minutes of the October 22, 2024 board meeting. Eleanor Grant seconded. Motion carried unanimously (10-0).

2. Q4 2024 FINANCIAL RESULTS PRESENTATION

Sarah Rodriguez presented Q4 and full-year 2024 financial results.

Key metrics reported:
  - Q4 2024 Revenue: $7.1 million (+22% YoY)
  - Full-year Revenue: $24.7 million (+18% YoY)
  - Annual Net Income: $3.5 million
  - Cash position: $6.8 million (no debt)
  - NRR (Net Revenue Retention): 112%
  - Customer count: 1,247 (from 986 at year start)

Thomas Vickers asked about the composition of the NRR figure. Sarah Rodriguez confirmed that expansion revenue from existing customers contributed $2.1 million in 2024, compared to $1.4 million in 2023.

Priya Mehta asked about the trajectory of CAC payback period. Sarah Rodriguez reported that average CAC payback improved from 14 months in 2023 to 11 months in 2024, driven by improved sales efficiency and higher average contract values in the Enterprise segment.

Board discussion: Thomas Vickers noted the 5.4% annual churn rate as a particular area of strength. The board agreed that continued investment in Customer Success is warranted.

Resolution: The board acknowledged the Q4 and full-year 2024 financial results as presented.

3. 2025 STRATEGIC PLAN REVIEW

Michael Chen presented the 2025 strategic plan under three pillars:

Pillar 1 — Product Leadership
David Kim outlined the 2025 product roadmap. Priorities include:
  - Acme AI Assistant launch (Q2 2025) — embedded AI capabilities within the Acme Platform
  - Acme Enterprise Suite (Q2 2025) — enterprise-tier pricing tier targeting companies with 500+ seats
  - API marketplace launch (Q3 2025)
  - Mobile app v2.0 (Q4 2025)
Total engineering budget proposed: $5.8 million (19% of revenue guidance midpoint).

David Kim noted that the team has been evaluating partnerships with two AI infrastructure providers, with a decision expected by February 28, 2025.

Pillar 2 — Market Expansion
Robert Martinez presented the 2025 sales strategy. Key elements:
  - Expansion into Canadian and UK markets (Q1 and Q2 2025)
  - Healthcare vertical go-to-market launch with dedicated Sales Engineer team (Q2 2025)
  - Financial Services vertical strategy (Q3 2025)
  - Sales headcount target: 43 sales representatives by December 2025 (from current 31)

Lisa Thompson noted that the marketing budget is proposed at $3.2 million, up from $2.4 million in 2024, with increased investment in field events, content marketing, and brand awareness campaigns in target verticals.

Pillar 3 — Operational Excellence
Jennifer Park presented the 2025 People strategy. Key initiatives include:
  - Hiring 40 to 50 net new employees across Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success
  - Launch of Acme Leadership Academy for manager development (Q1 2025)
  - Compensation benchmarking review (March 2025)
  - Employee Engagement Survey relaunch (Q2 2025)

Eleanor Grant asked about retention risk given competitive hiring conditions. Jennifer Park reported that Q4 2024 voluntary turnover was 9.2%, down from 13.4% in Q4 2023, attributing the improvement to the new equity refresh program and flexible work policy introduced in mid-2024.

4. 2025 FINANCIAL PLAN AND BUDGET APPROVAL

Sarah Rodriguez presented the proposed 2025 annual budget.

Revenue guidance: $30.0 million to $31.5 million
Operating expenses budget: $25.0 million
Expected operating income: $5.0 to $6.5 million
Planned capital expenditures: $480,000

Discussion: James Patterson asked whether the company should consider raising additional growth capital given the market opportunity. Michael Chen noted that the existing cash position of $6.8 million is sufficient to fund the 2025 plan without external financing, and that the board would revisit a potential growth round in Q3 2025 if expansion plans accelerate.

Thomas Vickers raised the topic of a potential strategic acquisition in the data infrastructure space, as mentioned in the CFO's Q4 report. Sarah Rodriguez confirmed that two targets are under preliminary evaluation, with no material discussions underway. The board authorized management to continue evaluating opportunities up to $3 million without prior board approval, consistent with existing delegation-of-authority policy.

Resolution: The board approved the 2025 annual budget as presented. Vote: 10-0.

5. COMPENSATION COMMITTEE REPORT

James Patterson, as Compensation Committee Chair, reported the committee's recommendations:
  - Annual merit increase pool of 4.5% for non-executive employees
  - Executive compensation adjustments to be presented at the March 2025 board meeting
  - New employee equity grant program effective January 1, 2025, with four-year vesting

Resolution: The board approved the merit increase pool of 4.5%. Vote: 10-0.

6. VENDOR CONTRACT RENEWAL — TECHSUPPLY SOLUTIONS

Sarah Rodriguez presented for approval the renewal of the cloud infrastructure management contract with TechSupply Solutions Inc. for the period January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2025.

Contract value: $180,000 (12-month term, same rate as 2024)
Scope: Cloud infrastructure management, 24/7 monitoring, security patching, and DR services

David Kim noted that the TechSupply Solutions team has maintained 99.97% uptime across Acme's production environments in 2024 and recommended renewal. Two alternative vendors were evaluated; TechSupply Solutions offered the best combination of price, SLA, and institutional knowledge.

Resolution: The board authorized execution of the TechSupply Solutions renewal contract. Vote: 10-0.

7. ANY OTHER BUSINESS

James Patterson reminded the executive team that the next board meeting is scheduled for March 26, 2025 at 9:00 AM CST. Agenda items to include Q1 preliminary results, executive compensation, and the AI partnership decision.

Michael Chen thanked the board for their continued support and reiterated confidence in the 2025 outlook.
