SECTION 2 — EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

2.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
Acme Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law. Our commitment to diversity and inclusion is central to who we are as a company.

2.2 At-Will Employment
Employment at Acme Corporation is at-will, unless a written agreement between the employee and Acme expressly provides otherwise. Either party may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.

2.3 Background Checks
All offers of employment are contingent upon completion of a satisfactory background check. Background checks are conducted by Acme's third-party vendor, ClearCheck Inc., in compliance with applicable law.

2.4 Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
All employees are required to sign Acme's Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Agreement as a condition of employment. This agreement covers trade secrets, customer data, proprietary software, and work product created during employment.

SECTION 3 — COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

3.1 Compensation Philosophy
Acme benchmarks compensation at the 65th to 75th percentile of our competitive market, defined as technology companies of similar scale in Austin, New York, and San Francisco. Compensation is reviewed annually each March, with adjustments effective April 1.

3.2 Paid Time Off (PTO)
Acme operates an unlimited PTO policy for all full-time employees. We believe that trust-based time off produces better outcomes than accrual systems. In practice, we encourage a minimum of 15 days off per year, including holidays. Employees are expected to coordinate with their manager and ensure coverage during planned absences.

Company-wide holidays (2025):
  - New Year's Day: January 1
  - Martin Luther King Jr. Day: January 20
  - Presidents Day: February 17
  - Memorial Day: May 26
  - Independence Day: July 4
  - Labor Day: September 1
  - Thanksgiving: November 27-28
  - Winter Break: December 24 - January 1, 2026

3.3 Health Insurance
Acme covers 100% of the premium for individual health insurance (medical, dental, vision) for all full-time employees and 80% of dependent premiums. Health insurance is through BlueCross BlueShield. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

3.4 Retirement
Acme offers a 401(k) plan through Fidelity Investments. Acme matches 4% of employee contributions, dollar-for-dollar, up to 4% of base salary. The employer match vests over a two-year period (50% after year one, 100% after year two).

3.5 Equity
Full-time employees are eligible to receive stock options in Acme Corporation. Grants are made at hire and may be refreshed annually based on performance and tenure. All equity grants vest over four years with a one-year cliff. Details are provided in individual offer letters and grant agreements.

3.6 Professional Development
Each employee receives a professional development budget of $1,500 per year, available for conferences, courses, books, or certifications related to their role. Budget resets January 1 annually.

3.7 Home Office Stipend
Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $750 to set up their workspace, and a monthly internet reimbursement of $50.

SECTION 4 — WORK ARRANGEMENTS

4.1 Hybrid Work Policy
Acme operates on a hybrid model. Employees based in Austin, New York, or San Francisco are expected to be in the office a minimum of two days per week (Tuesday and Thursday are our standard in-office days). Remote employees are expected to travel to the office for quarterly team weeks and company-wide events.

4.2 Remote Work
Employees may request full-time remote work arrangements. Approval is at the discretion of the employee's department head and requires a documented business justification. Full-time remote employees must be located in the continental United States.

4.3 Working Hours
Acme does not mandate specific working hours. We trust our employees to manage their time to deliver results. Core collaboration hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time are encouraged for team meetings and cross-functional collaboration.

SECTION 5 — PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

5.1 Performance Review Cycle
Acme conducts formal performance reviews semi-annually: in June (mid-year check-in) and in December (annual review). The annual review informs compensation adjustments effective the following April.

5.2 OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)
Acme uses OKRs to align individual, team, and company goals. OKRs are set at the beginning of each quarter and reviewed at the end. Employees set personal OKRs in collaboration with their manager.

5.3 Career Development
Career development discussions are held at least quarterly between employees and their managers. Acme maintains defined career ladders for Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Operations. The 2025 Acme Leadership Academy program, launching January 31, 2025, is available to all employees identified as current or emerging people leaders.

SECTION 6 — CODE OF CONDUCT

6.1 Professional Conduct
All employees are expected to conduct themselves with integrity, professionalism, and respect. This applies in all work settings, including virtual environments and external events.

6.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
Acme has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment of any kind. Harassment includes unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics, sexual harassment, and bullying. Any employee who believes they have experienced or witnessed harassment should report it to their manager, to any member of the People Operations team, or anonymously through Acme's reporting hotline at 1-800-555-0192.

6.3 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to the People Operations team. This includes outside employment, board memberships, or financial interests in companies that do business with Acme.

6.4 Data Privacy
Employees are responsible for handling customer and company data in accordance with Acme's Data Handling Policy. Sharing customer data outside of authorized systems is a violation of company policy and may result in immediate termination.
