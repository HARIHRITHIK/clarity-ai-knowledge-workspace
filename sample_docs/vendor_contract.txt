RECITALS

WHEREAS, TechSupply Solutions Inc. is in the business of providing cloud infrastructure management, monitoring, and security services to enterprise clients;

WHEREAS, Acme Corporation desires to engage TechSupply Solutions Inc. to provide such services with respect to Acme's production cloud infrastructure;

WHEREAS, the Parties previously entered into a Cloud Infrastructure Management Services Agreement dated January 1, 2024, and now desire to renew and supersede such agreement with this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the cloud infrastructure management services described in Exhibit A attached hereto.

1.2 "Infrastructure" means Acme Corporation's production cloud environment hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), including all compute instances, storage, networking, database services, and security configurations managed under this Agreement.

1.3 "Incident" means any unplanned interruption to or degradation of the Services.

1.4 "Critical Incident" means an Incident resulting in complete unavailability of the Acme production environment or a security breach affecting customer data.

1.5 "Service Level Agreement" or "SLA" means the performance standards set forth in Exhibit B.

1.6 "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party to the other that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

ARTICLE 2 — SERVICES

2.1 Scope of Services
TechSupply Solutions Inc. shall provide the following services to Acme Corporation:

  (a) 24/7/365 infrastructure monitoring across all production systems;
  (b) Automated alerting and on-call response for all Incidents;
  (c) Monthly security patch management and vulnerability remediation;
  (d) Quarterly infrastructure performance reviews and optimization recommendations;
  (e) Disaster Recovery (DR) plan maintenance and quarterly DR testing;
  (f) Capacity planning and scaling recommendations;
  (g) AWS cost optimization advisory with monthly reporting;
  (h) Annual security audit and penetration testing coordination.

2.2 Dedicated Account Team
TechSupply Solutions Inc. shall assign a dedicated account team to Acme Corporation consisting of:
  - Primary Account Manager: Rachel Okafor
  - Lead Infrastructure Engineer: Marcus Webb
  - Security Specialist: Hiroshi Tanaka

2.3 Change Management
All material infrastructure changes must be submitted via TechSupply Solutions Inc.'s change management portal with a minimum 48-hour advance notice for standard changes and 5-business-day notice for major changes. Emergency changes may be implemented immediately but must be documented within 2 hours.

ARTICLE 3 — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

3.1 Uptime Commitment
TechSupply Solutions Inc. guarantees production environment availability of 99.95% measured on a calendar-month basis, excluding scheduled maintenance windows. This corresponds to a maximum of 21.9 minutes of unplanned downtime per month.

3.2 Response Times

  Incident Severity  | Response Time | Resolution Target
  ───────────────────┼───────────────┼──────────────────
  Critical           | 15 minutes    | 4 hours
  High               | 30 minutes    | 8 hours
  Medium             | 2 hours       | 24 hours
  Low                | 8 hours       | 72 hours

3.3 SLA Credits
In the event TechSupply Solutions Inc. fails to meet the uptime commitment, Acme Corporation shall be entitled to SLA credits as follows:
  - Availability 99.5% to 99.95%: Credit of 10% of monthly fees
  - Availability 99.0% to 99.5%: Credit of 25% of monthly fees
  - Availability below 99.0%: Credit of 50% of monthly fees

SLA credits shall be applied as a deduction from the following month's invoice. Credits are the sole remedy for uptime failures.

ARTICLE 4 — TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 Term
This Agreement shall commence on January 1, 2025 and shall continue through December 31, 2025 (the "Initial Term"), unless earlier terminated pursuant to Section 4.2.

4.2 Termination for Cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within 30 days of receipt of written notice describing the breach.

4.3 Termination for Convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon 90 days' prior written notice to the other Party. In such event, Acme Corporation shall pay TechSupply Solutions Inc. for all Services rendered through the termination date.

4.4 Renewal
Unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods on the same terms and conditions.

ARTICLE 5 — FEES AND PAYMENT

5.1 Service Fees
In consideration for the Services, Acme Corporation shall pay TechSupply Solutions Inc. a monthly fee of $15,000, for a total annual contract value of $180,000.

5.2 Payment Terms
Invoices shall be issued on the first business day of each month. Payment is due within 30 days of invoice receipt. Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

5.3 Fee Increases
TechSupply Solutions Inc. may not increase fees during the Initial Term. For any renewal period, TechSupply Solutions Inc. may propose a fee increase not to exceed 5% per year, with 90 days' advance written notice.

5.4 Expenses
Pre-approved travel expenses incurred by TechSupply Solutions Inc. personnel in connection with on-site visits to Acme Corporation facilities shall be reimbursed within 30 days of submission. Expenses exceeding $1,000 require prior written approval from David Kim or Sarah Rodriguez.
