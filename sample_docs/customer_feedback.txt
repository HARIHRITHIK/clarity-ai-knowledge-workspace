EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Q4 2024 customer satisfaction results are the strongest in Acme Corporation's history. Net Promoter Score reached 58, up from 44 in Q4 2023 and 31 in Q4 2022. Overall satisfaction averaged 4.3 out of 5.0, with Product Quality and Customer Support receiving the highest individual ratings. Pricing and Reporting Capabilities were identified as areas with the greatest opportunity for improvement.

Key Metrics Summary:
  - Net Promoter Score (NPS):       58  (Industry Benchmark: 42)
  - Overall Satisfaction:          4.3 / 5.0
  - Product Quality Satisfaction:  4.6 / 5.0
  - Support Response Satisfaction: 4.4 / 5.0
  - Onboarding Satisfaction:       4.1 / 5.0
  - Pricing Satisfaction:          3.8 / 5.0
  - Reporting Satisfaction:        3.9 / 5.0
  - Survey Response Rate:         25.0% (312 of 1,247 active customers)

Customer: BrightPath Healthcare
Account Type: Enterprise | Annual Contract Value: $78,000
Contact: Dr. Angela Torres, COO
Rating: 4/5

Support communication during the incident could have been more proactive. That said, the underlying product quality is excellent."

Customer: Coastal Energy Partners
Account Type: Enterprise | Annual Contract Value: $68,000
Contact: Marcus Johnson, Director of IT
Rating: 4/5
Feedback: "We've seen meaningful productivity gains since go-live in August. The automation templates for our procurement approval workflows saved approximately $180,000 in labor costs in Q3 and Q4. My main feedback is that the reporting module lacks the customization depth we need for regulatory submissions. We currently export data to Excel for final report formatting, which is an extra step we'd like to eliminate."

Customer: Sterling Capital Management
Account Type: Enterprise | Annual Contract Value: $52,000
Contact: Rachel Kim, Managing Director
Rating: 5/5

When we identified a data synchronization edge case specific to our fund accounting workflow, the engineering team built a custom connector within three weeks at no additional charge. That kind of responsiveness is rare in enterprise software. Our team has adopted the platform at a 94% daily active usage rate."

Customer: Greenfield Manufacturing
Account Type: Mid-Market | Annual Contract Value: $18,000
Contact: Tom Harrington, Operations Manager
Rating: 4/5

But when comparing with competitors at renewal, the price increase discussion is always difficult. I'd encourage Acme to consider tiered pricing based on actual usage volume."
